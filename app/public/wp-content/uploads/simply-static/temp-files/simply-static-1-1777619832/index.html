Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a cost for attending? What does it cover?

Usually there is a cost to attend EQUIP, but this year there will be no cost.

Why do I need to register if there is no cost?

When you RSVP, you are helping us know how much food to order for lunch, as well as how many books and what language you prefer for the books you will be receiving.

Is there childcare?

On-site childcare is available for kids 3rd grade and under. We will be providing snacks for childcare, but we ask parents to bring snacks for their children if they have any allergy concerns.

Will there be a live-stream to watch online?

We do not plan to live-stream the conference. We encourage everyone to come out in-person for the full-experience.

Will this only be in English?

The sessions will be in English, but we do offer a live translation to text into Mandarin or Cantonese through a phone app.

Can I invite other people who do not attend LCBC?

Usually we make EQUIP open to other churches, but we are not doing that this year. We want to focus on how we are doing as a church when it comes to our ministries.

Where should I go when I arrive?

Upon arrival, check-in at the registration table in the MPR (the gray and white building) to get your name tag and books. Childcare will be located in the ETC (the red building).

I have another question.

Have more questions? Email us at information@lagunabaptist.org.
